Scanning

How do I scan?

Tap Start Scanning. A guided scan runs for one minute: walk slowly around the room or car, hold your iPhone close to bags, seats and pockets, then stand still for a few seconds. When it finishes, SafeScan shows what it found.

Why don't I see my own AirTag?

iOS hides the identity of AirTags from apps while they are near their owner. An AirTag only becomes detectable as an AirTag after it has been away from its owner for a while, which is the situation that matters for unwanted tracking. Your own AirTag in your pocket is not shown as a threat. Your iPhone also alerts you itself when an unknown AirTag moves with you.

What are "Other devices"?

Devices that don't say what they are, or that are far away. iOS hides the details of Apple devices such as Macs, iPhones and AirPods from apps, so your own devices usually appear here. Bluetooth also reaches through walls, so you may see devices from neighbours. SafeScan checks unidentified nearby devices for hidden AirTags automatically.

Why is there no exact distance?

Bodies, bags and walls weaken Bluetooth signals, so no app can measure distance precisely. SafeScan shows how close a device seems (Very close, Nearby, In range, Weak signal). To find a tracker, open it and tap Track This Device.

Finding a tracker

How does the finder work?

Walk slowly and stop every few steps. The percentage rises as you get closer, and the screen says when you're getting warmer or colder. Turn on Sound or Haptic for a beat that speeds up as you get closer. A "Where to look" list shows common hiding places.

Can I make a tracker beep?

Yes, for AirTags and other Find My items that are away from their owner: open the tracker and tap Play Sound. Apple allows this so people can find an unknown tracker. Other trackers can't be made to beep from an iPhone app.

How do I create a report for the police?

Open the tracker, or Dashboard › Tracker History › the tracker, and tap Export PDF Report. The report includes the tracker's details, why it was flagged, a map of the places it was seen and every sighting with its time and location.

Background Protection

What does it do?

With Background Protection on (Settings), SafeScan keeps watching for Samsung, Tile and Google trackers while it's closed, and alerts you if the same tracker is seen near you at 3 different places. It needs location access set to Always and notifications. Locations stay on your iPhone.

Does it detect AirTags in the background?

No. iOS doesn't let any app detect AirTags in the background. Your iPhone alerts you about AirTags itself.

I don't get alerts

Check Settings › SafeScan on your iPhone: Location should be set to Always and Notifications should be allowed. Bluetooth must be on. Low Power Mode can delay background checks.

Subscription

How do I restore my purchase?

Open SafeScan's subscription screen and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you used to subscribe.

How do I cancel?

Open the Settings app › your name › Subscriptions › SafeScan. Apple handles all payments and refunds; refunds can be requested at reportaproblem.apple.com.
