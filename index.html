Privacy Policy

Effective 14 November 2026

The short version
  • Bluetooth scanning happens on your iPhone. Information about nearby devices is never uploaded to us or anyone else.
  • Your scan history, safe list and settings stay on your device.
  • SafeScan does not access your location.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Purchases go through Apple; RevenueCat helps us check your subscription status.

This policy explains how SafeScan ("the app", "we", "us") handles information when you use the app. If you have any question, contact us at the email address at the end of this page.

1. Bluetooth scanning

SafeScan uses Bluetooth to detect signals that nearby devices broadcast openly, such as item trackers, headphones, watches and phones. While you scan, the app reads what these devices broadcast — for example a device name if one is sent, signal strength, manufacturer data and an identifier that iOS assigns to each device — to recognize the type of device, show roughly how close it is and rate its risk.

This happens only while the app is open, and only on your iPhone. SafeScan does not connect to the devices it detects, and we do not upload, collect or store this information on any server. Some detected devices may belong to other people; information about them is used only to show you results and is kept only in your scan history on your device. You can turn Bluetooth access off at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Bluetooth.

2. Magnetic field scanner

The Mag Scan feature reads your iPhone's magnetometer to measure magnetic fields near the phone. Readings are processed on your device while the scanner runs and are not saved or sent anywhere.

3. Data stored on your device

The following stays in the app's storage on your iPhone and is not sent to us:

  • Scan history: the date of each scan and, for detected devices, their name, device type, risk level and signal strength (up to your last 100 scans)
  • Devices you mark as safe
  • Your settings (for example sound and haptic feedback)

This data may be included in your own iCloud or computer backups of your iPhone, which are controlled by Apple and you. You can clear your scan history and remove devices from your safe list in the app, and deleting the app deletes all of this data from your device.

4. Purchases and subscriptions

SafeScan Premium is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes your payment; we never see your name, payment card or Apple Account details.

We use RevenueCat to check whether you have an active subscription. To do this, RevenueCat receives a random anonymous identifier created by the app, Apple's identifier for vendor (a device identifier that is the same only across apps from the same developer and cannot be used to track you across other companies' apps), your purchase and subscription history from Apple, and basic technical information such as your device model, iOS version, app version, language, country/region and IP address. This information is used only to provide and restore your purchases, not for advertising. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in iOS Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

5. Sharing and links

When you export a device report or share the app, you choose where it goes using the iOS share sheet; nothing is sent unless you choose to send it. Links such as "Rate Us" open the App Store, where Apple's own privacy policy applies. The app may also show Apple's standard rating prompt, which is handled entirely by Apple.

6. What we don't do

  • We don't access your location.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising identifiers.
  • We don't use analytics or crash-reporting services that track you.
  • We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

7. Children

SafeScan is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not ask for any personal information.

8. Your rights

Because your scan data stays on your device, you can view and delete it in the app or by deleting the app. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information about you. To make a request, including about data RevenueCat holds for your purchases, email us and we will help.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
