This policy explains how SafeScan ("the app", "we", "us") handles information when you use the app. If you have any question, contact us at the email address at the end of this page.

1. Bluetooth scanning

SafeScan uses Bluetooth to detect signals that nearby devices broadcast openly, such as item trackers, headphones, watches and phones. The app reads what these devices broadcast — for example a device name if one is sent, signal strength, manufacturer data and an identifier that iOS assigns to each device — to recognize the type of device, show roughly how close it is and rate its risk.

iOS hides the identity of Apple devices from apps. To recognize AirTags and other Find My items, SafeScan may briefly connect to nearby devices that don't identify themselves and asks only whether they offer Apple's unwanted-tracking service. It doesn't read or change anything else on them. When you tap Play Sound, SafeScan connects to that tracker and sends only the command that makes it play a sound.

Some detected devices may belong to other people. Information about them is used only to show you results and is kept only on your device. We do not upload, collect or store any of it on a server. You can turn Bluetooth access off at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Bluetooth.

2. Location

If you allow location access, SafeScan records where your iPhone was when it saw a tracker, so it can tell you if the same tracker has been with you at several places. With Background Protection on and location access set to Always, it also does this while the app is closed, using iOS's significant location changes to check when you move.

Locations are stored only on your iPhone, with each tracker's history, and are deleted automatically after 30 days. They are never sent to us or anyone else. SafeScan works without location access; it then can't tell whether a tracker has followed you between places. You can change location access at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services › SafeScan.

3. Background Protection and notifications

Background Protection is off unless you turn it on. While it's on, SafeScan listens in the background for the Bluetooth trackers iOS allows apps to hear (Samsung, Tile and Google trackers) and notifies you if one has been seen near you at several places. Notifications are created on your iPhone; nothing is sent to a server. You can turn Background Protection off in the app's Settings, and notifications off in iOS Settings.

4. Magnetic field scanner

The Mag Scan feature reads your iPhone's magnetometer to measure magnetic fields near the phone. Readings are processed on your device while the scanner runs and are not saved or sent anywhere.

5. Data stored on your device

The following stays in the app's storage on your iPhone and is not sent to us:

  • Scan history: the date of each scan and, for detected devices, their name, device type, risk level and signal strength (your last 100 scans)
  • Tracker history: for each tracker SafeScan has seen, the times it was seen, your approximate location at those times if you allowed location access, and its signal strength (kept for 30 days)
  • Devices you mark as safe
  • Your settings (for example Background Protection, sound and haptic feedback)

This data may be included in your own iCloud or computer backups of your iPhone, which are controlled by Apple and you. You can clear your scan history, delete any tracker's history and remove devices from your safe list in the app, and deleting the app deletes all of this data from your device.

6. Purchases and subscriptions

SafeScan Premium is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes your payment; we never see your name, payment card or Apple Account details.

We use RevenueCat to check whether you have an active subscription. To do this, RevenueCat receives a random anonymous identifier created by the app, Apple's identifier for vendor (a device identifier that is the same only across apps from the same developer and cannot be used to track you across other companies' apps), your purchase and subscription history from Apple, and basic technical information such as your device model, iOS version, app version, language, country/region and IP address. This information is used only to provide and restore your purchases, not for advertising. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in iOS Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

7. Reports, sharing and links

SafeScan can create a PDF report about a tracker, for example to give to the police. The report is created on your iPhone and includes the tracker's details and, if available, the times and locations it was seen. It is shared only if you choose to send it using the iOS share sheet. The same applies when you share the app. Links such as "Rate Us" and "Support" open the App Store or our website, and the app may show Apple's standard rating prompt, which is handled entirely by Apple.

8. What we don't do

  • We don't collect your location: it stays on your iPhone.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising identifiers.
  • We don't use analytics or crash-reporting services that track you.
  • We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

9. Children

SafeScan is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not ask for any personal information.

10. Your rights

Because your scan data, tracker history and locations stay on your device, you can view and delete them in the app or by deleting the app. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information about you. To make a request, including about data RevenueCat holds for your purchases, email us and we will help.
